# BhumiBandhan (ভূমি-বন্ধন)
### Smart Land Tech Protocol for Unified Land Verification & E-Governance in Bangladesh

> Developed for **LandTech 2026 Innovation Hackathon** (Organized by Department of CSE & CSE-TECH, Supported by Department of Law, Daffodil International University).

BhumiBandhan bridges the long-standing data silo between **Sub-Registry Offices (Ministry of Law)** and **AC Land / Union Land Offices (Ministry of Land)**. It introduces:
- **14-Digit Geo-ULPIN (Unique Land Parcel Identification Number)**
- **'কেনার আগে যাচাই' (Check Before You Buy) Citizen Risk Intelligence (0-100% Score)**
- **Baya Deed (বায়া দলিল) & Historical Khatian Chain Tracker (CS -> SA -> RS -> BS)**
- **Cryptographic Deed Fingerprinting (SHA-256) & Instant Anti-Double Selling Shield**
- **Bank Collateral & Digital Mortgage Lien Registry**
- **Dual Portal:** Citizen Portal & Officer (AC Land / Sub-Registrar) Administration Dashboard
